## The client

Demo Resort is a fictional small resort in Ubud, Bali, made up for the agency workflow demo. 11 villas
in three types: 6 Garden Villas, 4 Rice Field Villas and 1 Family Pool Villa. A restaurant, Demo
Kitchen, open to non-guests, and a spa, Demo Spa. Every page carries the site notice saying it is
fictional (PM decision).

## Who the site is for

- Primary visitor: couples and small families, aged 30 to 55, mostly from Australia and Europe.
- What they want: quiet, good food and the rice fields. Not party Bali.
- What they want to know before they enquire: not stated in the notes. Inferred from the villa
  details the owner gave: which villa fits them, the price per night, what is included (breakfast),
  how far it is from Ubud and the airport.
- Where they come from: search, per the owner's marketing person (queries below). Other channels not
  stated.

## Goal of the site

- Main action: send an enquiry through the enquiry form. The owner does not want WhatsApp on the site.
- Promise the owner made: "we reply to every enquiry within one working day".
- Booking engine or channel manager: none mentioned.

## Pages

- Home

## Voice

- Three words for the tone: simple, warm, calm. The owner: "keep it simple and warm, like how we talk
  to guests".
- A sentence the client wrote that sounds right: "Who we want: couples and small families who want
  quiet, good food and the rice fields, not party Bali."
- Words the client dislikes: "hidden gem", "paradise", "luxury" (on everything).

## Search

- Main queries: "villa with private pool Ubud", "rice field view villa Ubud", "family villa Ubud",
  "Balinese massage Ubud".
- Competitors the client compares themselves to: not stated.

## Must not say

From the owner's notes, these cannot appear on the site:

- "The most peaceful resort in Ubud" or any other superlative or ranking. Unverifiable.
- The 2023 TripAdvisor award, until the client sends the certificate or a link.
- Any health effect of a treatment. The owner wrote that the boreh scrub "detoxifies the body and
  cures jet lag": that is a medical claim and stays off the site.

Category risks for a hotel with a spa:

- Distances or drive times other than the two the owner gave (15 minutes by car to Ubud centre,
  about 1.5 hours to the airport). No "steps from", no walking times, no named landmarks.
- Star ratings, guest reviews, review scores, "award-winning".
- "Authentic", "traditional" as a quality claim, sourcing claims ("local", "organic", "farm to table")
  for the restaurant. "Traditional massage" is the owner's own description of the Balinese massage and
  may stay.
- Staff names, chef or therapist credentials, "family-run", founding year: none were given.
- Sustainability claims.
